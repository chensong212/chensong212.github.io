<p>Dr. Chen got his PhD of fluid mechanics from University of Chinese Academy of Science (<a href="https://english.cas.cn/">UCAS</a>) in 2016. Between 2013 to 2014, he studied in <a href="https://www.rwth-aachen.de/">RWTH Aachen University</a> (sponsored by UCAS and <a href="https://www.daad.de/en/">DAAD</a>). After conducting postdoc research in <a href="https://english.pku.edu.cn/">Peking University</a> and <a href="https://www.sustech.edu.cn/en/">SUSTech</a>, he joined Technical University of Munich (<a href="https://www.epc.ed.tum.de/en/aer/home/">TUM</a>) as Humboldt Fellow. Since 2022, he became a faculty member of <a href="https://ev.buaa.edu.cn/">Beihang University</a>.</p>
